HubSpot Reporting Training for Teams Across Hubs

Report builder, dashboards and attribution, trained on your own data rather than a sample account.

A dashboard is only as good as the records feeding it. Most reporting problems start below the chart: a lifecycle stage set by three different people, a source property left blank, a pipeline that changed mid-quarter and split a trend in two. I train teams to build reports they can maintain and to read them honestly, starting with the data underneath. The sessions run in your portal, on your properties, so what people learn applies the next morning.

Who this is for

This is for the people who own the numbers, and for the people who have to defend them.

  • Marketing and sales ops staff who maintain dashboards for others.
  • Team leads who prepare a weekly or board report from HubSpot.
  • A company whose reports disagree with each other, or with finance.
  • An admin who inherited dashboards nobody can explain.
  • A team adding attribution reporting and unsure where the source data comes from.

What the team learns

The program moves from the record to the report. Teams spend time on the layer most reporting training skips.

  1. Report builder. Single object, cross object and custom reports, with filters, date ranges and breakdowns chosen to answer one question well.
  2. Dashboards. One dashboard per audience, with the metric, owner and time window named, plus sharing and permissions that keep it readable.
  3. Attribution reports. What first touch, last touch and multi-touch each credit, and where the defaults mislead. Two write-ups on this site go deeper: multi-touch attribution in HubSpot and the HubSpot source attribution fix.
  4. Source data underneath. Properties, lifecycle stages, UTM handling, campaign membership and deal stages, and which of them a report depends on.
  5. Tracing a wrong number. Following a report back to the record, the property history and the workflow that changed the value.
  6. Where native reports stop. Calculated properties and the custom report builder when a standard report cannot express the question.

How sessions run

Sessions are hands-on. We build one report together, from the question to the filter to the check against the raw records, then the team builds the next one while I watch and correct.

A small group usually takes a half-day intensive. A larger team, or one spread across timezones, is better served by shorter workshops across a few weeks, with one audience per dashboard. View access helps me prepare with your actual properties and reports. If access is restricted, the sessions run on screen share only.

Sessions are live and remote, recorded on request, and quoted at a fixed price after a short scoping call.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you fix the data as well as train us?

Yes. What the sessions uncover can turn into a fixed-price cleanup or a retainer, agreed separately from the training.

Do we need a higher HubSpot tier for attribution reports?

Some attribution and custom reports depend on your HubSpot subscription, so I check what your tier includes during scoping. I check what your portal has during scoping, so the training stays on reports you can actually build and maintain.

Can you train on the dashboards we already have?

Yes. Reviewing what exists is often the first session: which reports are used, which are ignored, and which numbers disagree with each other.

Which hubs does this cover?

Sales Hub, Marketing Hub, Service Hub and Data Hub (formerly Operations Hub), plus the CRM records underneath them. Reporting questions cross hubs, so the sessions follow the data rather than one product line.

Is the training remote?

Yes. Everything runs live and remote, recorded on request, and scheduled around your team's working hours.
